How it works

Fixed in three steps.

You never leave the page. Highlight what you wrote, click once, and the fix shows up right where you were typing.

  1. 1

    Select the text

    Highlight a sentence, a paragraph or the whole message in any text box.

  2. 2

    Right-click, or press the shortcut

    Choose Fix Grammar with AI, or press CtrlShiftE (⌘⇧E on Mac).

  3. 3

    Done, in about a second

    A small pill shows while it works, then the corrected text replaces your selection.

What gets fixed

Mistakes corrected.
Your voice left alone.

Spelling, grammar and punctuation are fixed. Word choice, tone and style stay exactly as you wrote them.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no plan to upgrade to and no key to bring. The free service allows 10 corrections a minute and 200 a day per person, which is plenty for a day of email and chat.

What does it change, and what does it leave alone?

It fixes spelling, grammar and punctuation. It does not rewrite your sentences, swap your words or change your tone.

Where does it work?

Gmail, Outlook on the web, Slack, LinkedIn messages and comments, Facebook and Messenger are supported out of the box, plus most websites with ordinary text boxes, comment fields or editors. It runs in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium browsers.

Does it work in Google Docs?

Not yet. Docs draws its text on a canvas rather than as regular page text, so there is nothing for the extension to read. You will see a short message if you try.

What happens to my text?

Nothing leaves your browser until you ask for a correction. At that moment the selected text goes over an encrypted connection to a small server, which asks Google Gemini for the correction, with Groq as a backup. Nothing is stored and nothing is tied to you. Full privacy policy.

Can I change the keyboard shortcut?

Yes. Open the extension popup and choose Change, or go to chrome://extensions/shortcuts and set any combination you like.

Something did not work. Where do I report it?

Open an issue on GitHub. Bug reports and feature ideas are both welcome.
